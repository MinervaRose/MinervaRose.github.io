Sabrina Palis

AI Systems & Data Specialist · Independent Researcher · Higher-Education Technical Educator · Mentor & Consultant

Profile

Sabrina Palis is an AI systems and data specialist whose work brings together applied artificial intelligence, computational research, analytics and technical education. She designs auditable systems that help people examine complex information, understand uncertainty and make better decisions while retaining meaningful human oversight.

She holds a Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence with First Class Honours from the Udacity Institute of AI and Technology and Woolf. Her academic background also includes a Master of Arts in Education with Merit from the University of Nottingham and a Postgraduate Certificate in Education with Qualified Teacher Status in England and Wales. Alongside these degrees, she has completed advanced programmes in software development, deep learning, reinforcement learning, autonomous systems, data science, computer vision and generative AI through competitive scholarship initiatives.

Her current professional activities include higher-education technical education and curriculum design, technical mentoring in data analysis, business intelligence, machine learning and applied AI, independent research, and AI and data consulting. She also serves as a qualified juror for the French RS6891 professional certification in Generative Artificial Intelligence, assessing candidates' technical reasoning, methodology, documentation and communication.

She also accepts a limited number of private tutoring students on a highly selective basis. Her tutoring work has included the Da Vinci Program of Crimson Global Academy and Groupe Réussite, with experience supporting advanced learners and preparing students for selective French classes préparatoires entrance examinations.

Her technical and research interests include explainable and human-in-the-loop AI, decision-support systems, anomaly detection, scientific signal and time-series analysis, agentic workflows, analytics and visualisation, and AI-assisted scientific exploration. Across these areas, she places particular emphasis on evidence, uncertainty, reproducibility and human judgement.

Earlier in her career, Sabrina Palis worked in international relations and economic development, including positions with the Comité France Chine of MEDEF and Bretagne International. She now publishes technical articles, open-source systems, research-oriented prototypes and educational resources intended to make complex subjects more inspectable and communicable.

Writing and public scholarship

Sabrina Palis also publishes essays and technical reflections on Medium under the name Sabrina Jorgenson. This writing extends her work beyond software projects into questions of artificial intelligence, language, cognition, representation, mathematics and scientific thought.
