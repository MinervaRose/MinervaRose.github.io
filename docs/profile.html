Sabrina Palis

AI & Data Science Specialist · Independent Researcher · Educator · Mentor · Consultant

Profile

Sabrina Palis is an Artificial Intelligence and Data Science specialist whose work brings together research, education and software development. She develops AI systems, data science projects and educational resources with a particular interest in responsible artificial intelligence, scientific discovery and decision-support systems.

She is an alumna of the Udacity Institute of AI and Technology, where she completed the Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence in partnership with Woolf. Her academic background also includes the University of Nottingham, where she earned a Master of Arts in Education with Merit. Alongside these degrees, she has completed numerous advanced programmes in artificial intelligence, machine learning and data science through competitive scholarship initiatives.

Her professional activities include university teaching, professional mentoring, independent research, technical consulting and the assessment and certification of candidates as a qualified juror for the French RS6891 certification in Generative Artificial Intelligence.

She publishes research, technical articles, open-source software and educational resources intended to make complex technical subjects accessible to a broad audience.
