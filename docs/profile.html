Professional profile

A cross-disciplinary practice grounded in both technical formation and long-standing educational experience.

Sabrina Palis is an AI and data educator, mentor, certification juror, independent researcher, and applied AI systems practitioner. Her work brings together artificial intelligence, computational research, analytics, technical education, and human-centred system design.

She builds and investigates systems that make evidence, uncertainty, limitations, and reasoning more visible while preserving meaningful human judgement. Her interests include explainable and human-in-the-loop AI, decision-support systems, anomaly detection, scientific signal and time-series analysis, agentic workflows, analytics and visualisation, and AI-assisted scientific exploration.

She holds an MSc in Artificial Intelligence with First Class Honours from the Udacity Institute of AI and Technology and Woolf, an MA in Education with Merit from the University of Nottingham, an MBA from Universidad Isabel I, and a Licence in English from Université Rennes 2. She also holds a PGCE with Qualified Teacher Status.

Her technical development has been continuous since 2017, spanning software development, machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, autonomous systems, computer vision, cloud technologies, data science, and generative AI through structured programmes, specialist study, scholarships, and extensive project-based practice.

Her postgraduate business and management training also includes multiple ENEB títulos propios, including specialist study in Big Data. These programmes complement her formal university qualifications by extending her work into management, analytics, digital transformation, and applied business decision-making.

Independent research & open science

Exploratory computational work documented through inspectable public artefacts.

Her independent research explores how computation can help humans notice structure and meaningful signals in complex scientific and technical environments. Current themes include scientific triage, uncertainty-aware exploration, signal and time-series analysis, multimodal data, anomaly detection, human–AI collaboration, and scientific visualisation.

She favours research outputs that can be examined and revisited: repositories, notebooks, technical specifications, datasets, visualisations, reproducible experiments, and openly archived artefacts.
