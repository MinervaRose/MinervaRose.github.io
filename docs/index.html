Research / Areas of inquiry

Research interests

I'm interested in what computation can help us discover in complex scientific and technical settings, and how to distinguish a useful pattern from an overconfident interpretation.

01

Current investigations

Current lines of work include scientific triage, multimodal evidence, anomaly detection, AI-assisted investigation and the behaviour of agentic systems under human oversight. I document experiments through code, notebooks and technical reports where possible.

02

Developing directions

I'm deepening my knowledge of Earth observation and geospatial data science, particularly satellite-derived information and environmental monitoring. I'm also exploring spacecraft telemetry and digital forensics as further technical directions.
