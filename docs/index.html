Research direction

Independent, project-based research on AI systems for scientific triage, uncertainty-aware exploration, and human attention.

I explore how AI can help humans notice meaningful signals in complex scientific and technical environments: surfacing anomalies, connecting multimodal evidence, tracking uncertainty, and supporting disciplined investigation.

Current themes include scientific triage, AI mission control, human–AI discovery cockpits, agentic technical education, and multimodal frontier detection. The work is documented through reproducible repositories, technical specifications, notebooks, and research-oriented prototypes.

Teaching, mentoring, and communication

Technical education is both a professional practice and a test of whether systems and ideas are genuinely understandable.

  • Higher-education teaching and curriculum design across AI, data, cloud, business analysis, and digital systems.
  • Professional mentoring in data analysis, business intelligence, machine learning, Python, SQL, and applied AI projects.
  • Learning systems, visual resources, simulations, and agentic teaching tools that help learners reason rather than merely reproduce procedures.
